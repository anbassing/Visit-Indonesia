Frequently Addressed Inquiries

What is the standard lead time for BPOM cosmetic or health food product notifications? expand_more

Standard BPOM notification cycles typically span 4 to 9 months depending on ingredient novelty, microbiological lab testing protocols, and documentation accuracy. Our regulatory desk works directly with accredited domestic testing laboratories to minimize notification revisions and rejection loops.

How does VisitIndonesia.co.id verify local distributor solvency and compliance? expand_more

We perform a four-tier investigative audit: 1) Corporate registrar filings & beneficial ownership cross-checks; 2) Physical warehouse audits including cold-chain temperature telemetry verification; 3) Trade credit inquiries across incumbent principals; and 4) Tax and legal litigation search across Indonesian district courts.

Can we arrange customized multi-city industrial itineraries for board delegations? expand_more

Yes. Our Field Exploration team regularly manages private multi-city immersions combining chartered air transit, armored executive mobility, protocol-managed ministry visits, and confidential site inspections across Java, Sumatra, Bali, and Sulawesi.

Expedition Itinerary

4 Hours · Walk & Chauffeur
09:00 AM · Rendezvous & Reception

Meeting at Historic Cafe Batavia

Gather in the second-floor Churchill Lounge overlooking the square. Enjoy an opening briefing paired with a traditional spiced welcome elixir (Bir Pletok non-alcoholic botanical brew).

09:30 AM · Architectural Archive

Fatahillah Square & Underground Basements

Venture through the 1710 Dutch Stadhuis (City Hall). Descend into the preserved colonial sub-terranean holding chambers and study the intricate VOC teak joinery that has endured centuries of tropical humidity.

11:00 AM · Living Intangible Heritage

Master Wayang Guild & Shadow Demonstration

Private studio session with a 4th-generation puppet maker. Discover the water-buffalo parchment carving methods and manipulate an authentic hand-painted shadow puppet under expert instruction.

12:00 PM · Culinary Archive

Authentic Betawi Tasting Experience

Relax over a curated multi-dish luncheon featuring slow-cooked fragrant Soto Betawi with coconut-milk broth, spiced emping crackers, and artisanal Nasi Ulam herbal rice.

01:00 PM · Maritime Horizon

Spice Canal & Sunda Kelapa Schooner Port

Private air-conditioned transfer along Kali Besar canal to Sunda Kelapa harbor. Walk alongside hand-built wooden cargo Pinisi ships and witness the living spice port trade before expedition conclusion.
